# Substack Notes announcement (draft)

I made a little thing: **Staple**, a free Chrome extension that staples your week of Substack into one collection.

Pick your subscriptions (or a group of them, like "AI" or "Culture"), choose a date range, and Staple gathers every longform post you can read — free posts, plus any paid ones you subscribe to — into a clean PDF, an EPUB for your Kindle or e-reader, or a single HTML file.

Subscribe buttons and clutter stripped out, footnotes kept, and every piece links back to the original so you can still like, comment and restack.

It runs entirely in your browser: no account, no tracking, nothing sent anywhere.
